Wrenscan Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers paid plans for Wrenscan, made by Wrenscan LLC. We want you to be happy with Wrenscan. The free edition lets you try it on your own network before you pay anything.

Yearly plans

If Wrenscan isn't right for you, email us within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund. Later renewals can be refunded within 14 days of the renewal date if you haven't used the paid features since it renewed.

Monthly plans

You can cancel at any time and keep your plan until the end of the month you paid for. If Wrenscan didn't work for you in your first month, ask within 14 days of that first payment for a full refund.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from the customer portal (the "Manage subscription" link in Wrenscan, or the link in your receipt email). Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then Wrenscan returns to the free edition. Your scans and settings are kept.

How refunds work

Purchases are processed by our payment partner Polar, the merchant of record. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. When a refund is issued, the licence key stops working.

If the law where you live gives you more rights than this policy, those rights apply.
