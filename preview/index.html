Questions

Home network security scanner FAQ

Is Wrenscan really free?
Yes. The free edition checks up to 15 devices per scan, 15 scans a month, with plain-English explanations, one-click fixes and reports, and no time limit. Scheduled scans, alerts and the business tools need a paid plan.
What does a home network security scanner do?
It finds every device connected to your network and checks each one for problems attackers use, such as open admin pages, old file sharing, weak encryption and outdated software with known vulnerabilities.
Does Wrenscan stop ransomware?
No, and nothing that only scans can; Windows Defender does the blocking. Wrenscan checks whether the protections against ransomware and password theft are switched on, tells you which are off, and on Home Plus turns them on in one click with an undo. It also checks whether your files have a backup, and spots the traces some common scams leave behind, like a fake CAPTCHA that had you paste a command.
Is it safe to install?
Yes. The installer and every program file are digitally signed, updates are signed and verified before they install, and the download page lists the file's SHA-256 so you can check it.
Will scanning break anything?
Wrenscan only looks: it never runs exploits, tries passwords or sends anything to printers. If you have fragile equipment, tick "Never scan this device" and Wrenscan will list it but leave it alone.
Does Wrenscan send my data anywhere?
No. Scans, devices and findings stay on your PC and are never sent to us. Wrenscan goes online only to check for updates, download public security data, check a paid licence, look up whether your public internet address is on spam blocklists (DNS lookups; can be turned off), and (if you set them up) send your alerts.
Do I need to be technical?
No. Every finding is explained in plain English with step-by-step fixes, and many problems on your PC can be fixed with one click and undone later.
Does it work on Mac or Linux?
Wrenscan runs on Windows 10 and 11 today, and it scans Macs, phones, printers and every other device on your network. A free Mac beta is coming soon: join the Mac list.
When are the paid plans available?
Home Plus and the business plans are coming soon. Join the founding members list or email support@wrenscan.com to hear when they launch.
How do I get help?
Email support@wrenscan.com and we'll get back to you. Include what you were doing and any error message you saw.

Only scan networks you own. Wrenscan is for your own home or business network, or one you have written permission to test. It never runs exploits or tries to break in.
