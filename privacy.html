Wrenscan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Wrenscan LLC ("we") makes Wrenscan, a network security scanner that runs on your own computer. The short version: your scans, findings, device lists and settings stay on your computer. We do not collect them, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your computer

Everything Wrenscan finds: the devices on your network, their addresses, names and makers, open ports, software versions, vulnerabilities, fix history, scan schedules, and your settings. It is stored in Wrenscan's data folder on your computer and is never sent to us.

What the app sends over the internet, and why

WhenSent toWhat is sent
Checking for updates (a few seconds after start, then about every 6 hours; can be turned off)Our download site, hosted by GitHub PagesA request for the update file. It identifies only the app version, edition and operating system. A random install ID used for gradual rollouts is created and kept on your computer and is never sent.
Downloading security data (vulnerability advisories, known-exploited lists, exploit likelihood scores, device-maker lists)Public sources: Google's OSV database, CISA, FIRST/Empirical Security (EPSS), the IEEE and WiresharkA download request. Nothing about your network is sent.
Activating or checking a licence key (paid plans only; at most about once a day)Polar, our payment partnerYour licence key, our store ID, and a label such as "Wrenscan on Windows". No computer name, user name or scan data.
Alerts you set up yourself (email, Slack, Discord, webhooks)Only the destinations you enterThe alert you configured, such as a summary of new findings. Nothing is sent unless you set it up.

Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address to the server that receives them. We do not receive the logs of GitHub, Google, CISA or the other public sources.

When you buy a plan

Purchases are handled by Polar (polar.sh), the merchant of record, under Polar's own privacy policy. Polar collects your payment details; we never see your card number. Polar shares with us what we need to run your account: your name, email address, country, what you bought, and your licence key and its activations. We use this to provide the product, support and receipts, and keep it as long as your account is active and as required for tax and accounting records.

When you contact us

If you email support, we keep your messages and anything you choose to include (for example a scan report) to help you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Our websites

  • wrenscan.com is built and hosted with Wix. Wix uses cookies, including for site analytics, as described in its cookie banner and privacy policy; you can decline non-essential cookies in that banner.
  • downloads.wrenscan.com, where the installer and update files live, is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits under GitHub's privacy statement. We set no cookies and use no analytics or advertising trackers there.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it except with the service providers named above, or when the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off automatic update checks in the app, and delete all local data by uninstalling Wrenscan and removing its data folder. To access, correct or delete information we hold about you (for example purchase or support records), email us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, which we will honour.

Children

Wrenscan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes say so in the app or on our website.
